title: "Always look on the bright side of life"
date: 2026-07-28
---

«If life seems jolly rotten, there's something you′ve forgotten. And that′s to laugh and smile and dance and sing!» Thankfully, there's humor in this world. Thanks to Monty Python!


My situation didn’t really change, but the construction work in my apartment was finally finished, and then the first heatwave came. This helped me to change my state of matter from solid to liquid. I became more and more active, and the more active I became, the more ideas came to mind.


The learner’s group, which was actually planned to start in spring, was postponed to July. In June, my self-esteem was rather low, and I didn’t feel capable of teaching WordPress to beginners. I had to pull myself together and complete my tasks, such as writing an e-mail to all the participants and putting the links to the German content on our website.


I asked the people in my choir if I should create a new website for us, and they agreed. So I had to install a local version of WordPress and start to work on several new designs that I could present to the others.


Also, I wanted to intensify my Ruby studies. In my dark time, I wasn’t joining the lessons regularly. I hardly remembered my last task, when I had to program an IRC bot. Suddenly, the idea came to my mind that I could write an interactive story, where the user is given two options for how the story goes on, and he can choose option a or b. 

The story became increasingly convoluted, and it became almost impossible to keep track of everything. Next Monday (the Rubymonstas meet on Mondays), I told my Ruby teacher to open libera.chat and presented him my interactive story. When he looked at the code, he was even more astonished than me, that this code works. But it did!


And I had a greater plan. I wanted to put the story online. But this seems to be rather tricky, so I’ll write about that sometime in the distant future.
