---
title: "No more teaching"
date: 2026-09-04
---
Last Monday, I definitely decided to teach no more, no matter what the setting looks like. 
Since I started teaching in our study group “Start with WordPress”, my mood got worse again. 
My thoughts kept circling back to the fact that we are not doing things as we originally designed the study group. 
The original idea was that the students learn for themselves with the lessons on learn.wordpress.org and that we are just coaches who answer questions and give advice. 
At the end of the day, we were not coaches but teachers. There was never enough time to teach everything we wanted, 
and the students had very little time for asking questions. 
I tried to accept that things are going this way for now and do my best, but I was not able to stay in a good mood.


On Tuesday last week, I was finally teaching using and creating patterns. 
Although I was prepared very well, some things didn’t work what made me totally confused. 
I wasn’t able to explain everything carefully anymore. This experience was rather frustrating.


The next day, I tried to focus on the following evening and prepare myself even better. 
I quickly realised that we won’t be able to do all the things that are planned in the ninety minutes that are scheduled. 
So, I wrote a message to my co-moderator with my analysis and a proposal on how to solve this. I didn’t get an answer. 
That reminded me of the hundreds of moments in my previous teaching job when I was frustrated. 
I felt angry and helpless, and the memories made it impossible for me to get in a good mood again.


In the end, I wrote to the WordPress team that I was worried about my mental health and therefore quit the moderator team. 
This was surely a necessary step, and afterwards I felt better again. 
After all, the people in this group reacted very sympathetically.


But it also made me feel sad that I had to end something that I actually wanted to finish successfully. 
And now I am worrying about how I will ever be able to be successful in a job if working together with other people is so difficult for me.


For now, I am trying not to think too much about what went wrong, but to focus on other things. 
Last Monday, after sending the sad message, I was working on an exercise from the programming course and continued to learn HTML and CSS. 
In the evening, I went to Rubymontas and had a good time there. 
I have now finished a CV I wrote in HTML and CSS, and I published it on GitHub. [Lebenslauf](https://valeriewandeler.github.io/lebenslauf/)
I am continuing to work on the website for my choir. 
I am involved in creating an evaluation form for the WordPress study group as well. 
So, I am not out of the group completely.


And there is something that is connected to my previous job as a teacher, but in a positive way. 
I reactivated a tool that I used quite often when I was a teacher. 
It is called Wordwall, and it makes it possible to create learning games very easily. 
In one of my previous posts, I wrote about my wish to have an app like Duolingo for programming languages. 
As this wish cannot be realised very quickly, I started to create learning games for Ruby with Wordwall. 
That was fun, and it helped me to learn some things better.
