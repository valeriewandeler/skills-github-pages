title: "A dark time"
date: 2026-07-26
---
After this job interview, I bought a book about math and started to learn. I was fully motivated. And full of confidence in my grand plan.


Who said it was easy? – It wasn’t that easy. You don’t just need a brain to study. You need a living as well.


Unfortunately, a few weeks after the interview, I had to tell the company where I wanted to do the internship 
that I had to postpone my plans by a year. I depend on financial support for my studies, and the responsible office said 
that first, I have to work full-time for six months to prove that I handle pressure. So I started to look for an internship 
I could do before I start studying.


And an other few weeks later, a doctor I had never seen in my life decided I could just go back to my previous job. 
That means a job that brought me to psychiatry twice. No way! I’m not gonna do this.


This was also the time when my kitchen and bathroom were under construction. The construction workers made a lot of noise, 
the heating was turned off, and the weather was still quite cold. A toilet was in the backyard, and I was suffering from diarrhea. 
I spent most of the time in bed with the blanket over my head.
