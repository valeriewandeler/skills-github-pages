14h - The WordPress beginner learning group started on August 28. Today will be the fourth evening. And I am going to be one of the two presenters and teachers, together with Andy. This is a strange feeling for two reasons.

First, I’ve been telling people for more than a year that I never ever will be teaching again. And now I am playing the role of the teacher again. I think I am quite a good teacher in the sense that I am good at explaining things. When I was 14 years old, it took me half an hour to teach my five-year-old sister how to read. And once I taught a little boy swimming in just ten minutes. When I practised Karate and was wearing a yellow belt, I had already started teaching beginners with white belts. But there is a crucial skill that I lack. I am surely somewhere on the autism spectrum and neither really interested in other people, nor in curricula.

In our learning group, we are team teaching. So I had to propose my ideas to my partner, Andy, and, of course, there was a discussion about the curriculum. I know exactly what the right thing to do is, and having to discuss things that are true facts is a very hard thing for me.

Second, I am now working on a Website for my choir, and this is much more interesting for me than seeing what websites other people build. While I am working on my own website, I constantly try to do things that don’t work right away, and I fear that this evening all the things that I explain them don’t work. This risk I could reduce to a minimum. I prepared an example page for my ten-minute input where everything I have to explain is already done. So, I just ask the students to guess with which kind of blocks I’ve created the header, footer and content and then show them the blocks in the list view.

So now, everything’s fine? No, I'm prepared only in terms of content, but not emotionally. It took me a lot of time to prepare the example page, and now, I expect everybody to freak out about it - which they are not going to do. So, I will need to deal with my frustration. Also, the students will have to present their projects, what they have done so far. Maybe I should ask Claude to come up with a few motivational phrases for me. This way, I'll really be prepared, and the worst-case scenario won't happen. And tomorrow, I can finally spend my time again on more pleasant things such as Ruby, HTML or the Website for my choir.

18.00h - I'm bent over the toilet, emptying my stomach. Now that it's empty, everything should be fine.

19.00h - I'm about to start the class, feeling confident. Then I excuse myself and rush to the restroom. Five minutes later, I apologize to the class. I'm sick and can't teach.

20.00h - My mind keeps going in circles—why? Is it because of the sandwich I ate? Did my roommate pass it on to me (he was sick on Sunday)? Is my body trying to tell me that I really should stop teaching?

22.00h - Finishing this post.
