One heatwave followed the other while I was focusing on several tasks (learning Ruby, HTML and CSS, creating a website for my choir, getting used to GitHub, creating this blog and continuing to write posts, installing a plugin on my website to post the blog there as well).


There were no distractions, such as group therapy, choir practice or my flatmate. I was all alone in my sister's apartment who was on vacation offering her cats an all-you-can-eat buffet. I followed the Japanese concept Ikigai, which means to live in the moment, take one step at a time, be nourished by your dream and guided by your goal. That’s what I understood about Ikigai. Living completely ~~behind my laptop~~ in the moment, I almost forgot my birthday.
In previous years, I was getting sad on my birthdays, because it is vacation time and nobody is there to celebrate. This year, I decided to celebrate my birthday on my own. I was listening to Amy’s song «Valerie» (until Spotify told me that tomorrow I’ll be able to choose a song again or I have to sign up for premium) and baked a Rublycake, which I would bring to Rubymonstas the next
day. A Rubly cake contains Rüebli (=carrots) and has a Rubymonsta on top.

###################################################################
So far, I’ve been looking back on the past year, which is why I’ve been writing in the past tense. Right now, I’m switching to writing about the present moment – Ikigai - and using the present tense.
###################################################################


What can I do to master this challenge? I think I need repetition. It’s not good to be learning new things constantly without refreshing what you’ve already learnt. Another student at Rubymonstas, who has been in the course much longer than me, is doing all the exercises a second time. I am trying to do at least the exercise from last Monday again. The result is that my mood is getting lower and lower. The exercise is still as hard as when I did it the first time.


I think I should better do repetition with more simple exercises. When I want to refresh a foreign language that I have already learnt on a high level, but didn’t use for a long time, I like to learn with Duolingo. You get easy tasks and can do a lot of exercises in a short time. This way, you get into a routine.


Yes, that’s the solution! My Ruby teachers have to develop a Duolingo for programming languages!!! Wait... Maybe they don’t want to do this. Or, it will take too much time to do this. I need a solution that works right now. So, I ask Claude for help. Claude recommends ~~exorcism~~ exercism.org. I go there and register and click on «beginner». Then I get to jiki.io, where I can learn basics about coding in general. But I want to learn Ruby in specific. So I go back to where you chose your level and click on «junior developer». Now, I can choose from a wide variety of languages, and for now I chose Ruby, of course. And I start. ~~Exorcism~~ Exercism is not really the same thing as Duolingo, but it is free and, until now, without ads. So, I’ll give it a chance.
