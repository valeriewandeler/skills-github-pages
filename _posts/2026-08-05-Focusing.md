One heatwave followed the other while I was focusing on several tasks (learning Ruby, HTML and CSS, creating a website for my choir, getting used to GitHub, creating this blog and continuing to write posts, installing a plugin on my website to post the blog there as well).

There were no distractions, such as group therapy, choir practice or my flatmate. I was all alone in my sister's apartment who was on vacation offering her cats an all-you-can-eat buffet. I followed the Japanese concept Ikigai, which means to live in the moment, take one step at a time, be nourished by your dream and guided by your goal. That’s what I understood about Ikigai. Living completely ~~behind my laptop~~ in the moment, I almost forgot my birthday.
In previous years, I was getting sad on my birthdays, because it is vacation time and nobody is there to celebrate. This year, I decided to celebrate my birthday on my own. I was listening to Amy’s song «Valerie» (until Spotify told me that tomorrow I’ll be able to choose a song again or I have to sign up for premium) and baked a Rublycake, which I would bring to Rubymonstas the next
day. A Rubly cake contains Rüebli (=carrots) and has a Rubymonsta on top.
