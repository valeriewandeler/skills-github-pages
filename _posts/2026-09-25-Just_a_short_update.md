title: "Just a short update"
date: 2026-09-25
---

The last few weeks were turbulent. Suddenly, I hoped I could do an internship, 
and then it was gone again. My situation would swing from hopeful to desperate every few days.


Still in August, I applied for a part-time job at 20% at a web platform called philosophy.ch. 
I wasn’t optimistic about getting a job there, and I wasn’t disappointed when they told me they currently don’t have any 
financial resources to employ people. The decline came shortly after I met with my job counsellor, 
who told me to start an internship or a course in November. So I answered the guy from philosophy.ch 
that I could do an internship in IT and they wouldn’t have to pay me for this. They were very happy about this, 
because their website is ten years old and really needs a better UX design.


I told all the people who are involved in my case about this, and three days later, I was informed that there really is a possibility 
that I can do an internship there. On September 17th, I had a talk with all three guys who work for philosophy.ch. 
It was a very good talk. I felt that finally things are moving in the right direction. The guy from IT is willing to teach me 
everything I need to start supporting him. I convinced them that for me this would be a great opportunity 
and that I was really eager to learn and do as much as possible. After the talk, I wrote an e-mail to my counsellor at the job centre, 
and I was as happy as I hadn’t been for a very long time.


The next day, her answer came. She told me it wasn’t yet clear whether I could do the internship, and that she first had to confirm 
who would support it financially. In the meantime, I should start with a program, which is obviously no problem to finance. 
In this programm, I have to do things like rubbing the paper off a piece of cardboard to reveal a picture underneath. 
Euphemistically, this activity could be described as meditative.


Luckily, I spent much time studying philosophy and understood that it’s not useful to ask the question Why?. 
Well, for now, I spend four hours a day exercising patience and in the afternoon I open my laptop and do more interesting things. 
These are: publishing the new website of my choir. Enhance my online CV. Learn Ruby, Git, Ux Design and whatever comes my way.


I understand that it is difficult for people who work in offices to encourage a 48-year-old woman to go into IT. 
Last spring, I asked all my advisers if I should become a carpenter (then I would go to the gym more often and get a driver’s licence) 
or go into IT (then I would learn programming languages and math). They told me to go into IT and that's what I am doing. 
As long as no one presents me a more feasible plan for my future.
